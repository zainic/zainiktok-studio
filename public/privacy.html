AT A GLANCE

Your videos and account access remain under your control. This policy describes what the app handles, when it sends data to TikTok, and how you can manage or request deletion of stored information.

01Who operates this service

Zainiktok Studio is a self-hosted tool operated by [OPERATOR OR LEGAL ENTITY] (“we” or “us”). We are responsible for how we configure and operate this deployment of the Service. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to [CONTACT EMAIL].

Where the Service is deployed by a separate organization, that hosting organization may be the controller of personal information in its own deployment. Update this document to identify the correct responsible party before publishing.

02Information we process

Depending on the features you use, the Service may process:

  • Connected TikTok account information: TikTok account identifier, granted permission scopes, access and refresh tokens, and creator privacy options returned by TikTok's API.
  • Video and publishing content: videos you upload or are authorized to download, file names and sizes, captions, titles, source links, scheduling times, privacy choices, and publication results or errors.
  • Workspace preferences: selected time zone and account-connection state.
  • Session and operational data: session cookie, login-attempt information held temporarily in server memory, and error or diagnostic information generated during operation. Your hosting provider or reverse proxy may independently record IP addresses and request logs depending on its configuration.

The included dashboard does not collect payment-card details, and sample analytics displayed in the interface do not represent real TikTok performance data.

03How and why we use information

We use the information described above to provide and secure the workspace, authenticate the connected account, organize uploaded content, retain scheduled jobs, attempt TikTok publication at the instructed time, report job status, perform authorized link downloads when enabled, and troubleshoot errors.

Any legal basis required by applicable privacy law, such as contractual necessity, consent, or legitimate interests, must be confirmed by the actual operator and local legal requirements before public launch. You may revoke TikTok authorization or disconnect the account from the workspace.

04TikTok and other third parties

When you explicitly connect an account, the Service exchanges authorization information with TikTok. When you instruct a post, relevant video content and settings are transmitted to TikTok's Content Posting API for processing under TikTok's own terms and privacy practices.

If link downloads are enabled, the server may request the public URL using the configured yt-dlp tool. Your hosting provider may process infrastructure data. We do not include third-party advertising or analytics tracking in the provided application code. Any additional services installed by an operator must be disclosed here.

05Cookies and browser storage

The application uses a first-party zstudio session cookie to maintain a signed-in workspace session. The cookie is configured as HttpOnly and SameSite=Lax, with the Secure attribute enabled when configured for HTTPS deployment. Session state is held in server memory and normally expires after up to 24 hours or when the server restarts or you sign out.

The provided application does not depend on third-party advertising cookies. If you add website analytics or external services later, revise this Policy and implement any required notice or consent.

06How information is stored and protected

Videos and publishing records are stored on the host server; application records are stored in a local SQLite database. TikTok OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using a server-held encryption key. Access to the workspace is password-protected.

Security also depends on the deployment operator maintaining HTTPS, appropriate file permissions, backups, software updates, and restricted server access. No system can guarantee absolute security.

07How long data is retained

Uploaded videos, completed downloads, and scheduled-post records remain on the server until they are deleted using available workspace controls or by the operator, subject to operational and legal requirements. Disconnecting a TikTok account removes the locally stored TikTok authorization record but does not automatically delete existing videos or scheduled posts.

Backups or infrastructure logs may be retained under the deployment operator's own retention procedures. Before publishing, the operator should document its actual backup and log retention periods: [BACKUP / LOG RETENTION DETAILS].

Disconnecting Zainiktok Studio does not necessarily revoke permissions at TikTok; you may separately review connected app permissions in TikTok's account settings. The operator should ensure a functioning request and deletion process before public launch.
